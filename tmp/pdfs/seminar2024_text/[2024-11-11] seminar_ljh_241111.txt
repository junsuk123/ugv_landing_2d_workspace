INTRODUCTION
• Research background
• State estimation of system is important
• Smoothing filter (SF)
• Higher state estimation accuracy compared to a regular Kalman filter
• Limitation: Difficult to implement in real time
• Real-time smoothing filter (RSF)
• Window formation method (via selection of appropriate window length)
• Limitation: Due to formal and structural limitations, there is no gain in the 
accuracy of current state estimation
• Propose: Real-time constrained smoothing filter (RCSF)
• Target: break the limitation of RSF in aspect of filter equation
• Process
• Define a prior information for the backward filter
• Form constraints based on the prior information
