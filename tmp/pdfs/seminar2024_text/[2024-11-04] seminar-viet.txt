Conclusion
18
• We conduct a comprehensive analysis against three existing 
state-of-the-art methods using the real-world dataset.
• We propose
⚬ A practical bias estimation to enhance acceleration estimation from the 
accelerometer.
⚬ An adaptive parameter rule is designed to improve ego- velocity estimation 
accuracy.
⚬ An acceleration-based Constraint method for robust Radar Ego-Velocity 
Estimation (CREVE). This framework functions as a submodule within a radar-
inertial odometry system.
