Conclusion
21
• We have proposed DeRO, a framework of dead reckoning 
based on radar odometry with tilt angle measurements. 
• Our Approach
⚬ Avoid double integration of accelerometer in computing odometry.
⚬ Compensate Doppler velocity scale factor error.
• Rigorously evaluation using open-source dataset and toolbox.
• Make our source code openly available for the benefit of the 
research community.
