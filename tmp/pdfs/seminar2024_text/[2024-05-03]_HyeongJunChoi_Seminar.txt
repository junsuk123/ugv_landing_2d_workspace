BACKGROUND (1/2)
• As the count of unmanned platforms rises along with the frequency of missions carried out indoors, 
the demand for indoor positioning escalates.
• Efforts are underway to explore platform positioning techniques within indoor settings through 
avenues like vision, wireless signals, and map-based approaches.
• Utilizing wireless signals offers benefits in terms of reliability and scalability, while employing 
UWB (Ultra-Wideband) for positioning can yield highly precise measurements of position.

BACKGROUND (2/2) 
• In static or consistently occupied areas, employing the wireless signal-based positioning method 
can yield accurate measurements by installing four or more anchors.
• Yet, it could prove challenging to deploy multiple anchors in continuous indoor environments or 
areas with sporadic platform demands.
• Hence, positioning was achieved by cutting down the necessary anchors from four or more to just 
two.
• And then, a technique for acquiring positioning data via 2d -Lidar and INS information is 
introduced.

BILATERATION POSITIONING TECHNIQUE(4/5)

location.

location may not perfectly align.

DOP ANALYSIS (1/3)
• DOP measures how good the setup of satellites is when using satellite navigation. A lower DOP 
value means the measurement is more precise and of better quality
• DOP includes different types like HDOP (horizontal), VDOP (vertical), PDOP (position), TDOP 
(time), and GDOP (position and time). This study focused on PDOP, which was evaluated based on 
where anchors were placed
• Since you can't calculate DOP with just 2 anchors, 2 more anchors positioned at right angles to the 
first two were added. This way, PDOP was calculated using a total of 4 anchors.

CONCLUSION
• In indoor positioning using wireless signals, location data is typically derived from distance 
measurements using four anchors.
• In this study, situation when multiple anchors are difficult to install, an attempt was made to obtain 
distance information using two anchors and location information using a rangefinder and inertial 
sensor
• DOP analysis confirms that the quality of measurements improves as the anchor moves away from 
the axis on which it is installed.
• Based on this, through trajectory scenarios, results were obtained within about 50cm at 6.5m in 
straight and curved trajectories on the installation axis.
• By comparing the given errors in the mobile section and the additional cost of installing two more 
anchors, it is believed that the method proposed in this study can be used if there are advantages.
